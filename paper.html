maTumbo Living Reality — White Paper

Don't display information. Materialize it. A private, local-first 3D world where every system is a place you can walk into.

The idea

maTumbo Living Reality is a single local runtime that turns abstract systems — contracts, ledgers, rooms, identities, markets — into navigable 3D space. You don't read about a contract; you stand in front of it, open it, stake on it, resolve it. The 3D world is a projection of local state: everything you see is computed on your machine, from your machine's data.

Every object in the world is mutable: tap anything to move it through all three dimensions, rotate it, or stretch it. Where you leave it is where it stays — arrangements persist across snapshots and replay.

How it runs

  • :8080 — the full 3D demo (index.html). Three.js runs locally from vendored files; no CDN, no network calls.
  • :8181 — this paper (paper.html). Pure document, zero 3D dependency, always loads.
  • Both served from the same checkout with python -m http.server. Never open the files directly with file:// — the 3D modules require a server.

Hard boundaries

  • Local simulation only. Nothing here is a wallet, a chain, custody, or settlement.
  • No real money, no wagering, no mainnet. Rehearsal credits are fictional and zero-valued.
  • Luna runs on a deterministic local grammar — no cloud AI, no speech recognition, no network.
  • The camera layer (Gesture Lens, in progress) processes video on-device only. Camera off by default; no recording, no upload.
  • The 3D world is a projection. It is never authority over money, identity, or agreements.
