A room that responds locally
The trained rule estimates movement cost. Neighbor-to-neighbor updates grow a guidance field toward a goal. Blocking a cell forces the field to rebuild.
369 trained parameters, synthetic task, pinned model. Local organization is emergent from repeated updates; no general intelligence, free-form room generation or online retraining is claimed. No rule moves your work without an edit.
