Looking for your saved Field Lab…
Use the original matumbo-field-lab-v04.html only when no saved app is available. It is read locally, not uploaded. No new app download is required.

Start with touch: leave the camera off, press a labelled ring and drag it. A gold ring means HELD. For camera control: open Camera / AR, start Front + hands, show an open hand, then aim, pinch, move and release.

Modes, privacy and limits

Grab node moves a simulation node. Move field moves the whole field. View turns your viewpoint. Room AR places the field on a detected surface on supported devices; use center aiming and tapping there, not simultaneous camera hand gestures.

The neural hand model is pretrained. Optional calibration fits your pinch thresholds and can save only those thresholds locally. Frames are processed in this browser. The hosted hand assets download from this same site; compatibility tracking may use the pinned original providers. No microphone, frame uploads or recording.

This is a software field viewed through your screen, not light projected into empty air. Phone performance and room tracking depend on the device. The update preserves the original field state and physics.
